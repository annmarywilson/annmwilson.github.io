ABOUT

I’m a Data Science Manager with 10+ years of experience leading analytics, forecasting, and decision science across media, consumer, and enterprise platforms.

I specialize in translating ambiguous business questions into clear analytical plans—owning the full lifecycle from opportunity sizing and modeling through implementation and success measurement.

My work sits at the intersection of rigorous statistics, scalable data platforms, and executive storytelling— helping teams move faster, make better decisions, and measure real-world impact.

FOCUS AREAS

I lead teams that build decision-ready analytics: forecasting and scenario models to plan ahead, causal and experimental methods to measure what actually works, and analytics platforms that make insights reliable, repeatable, and easy to adopt.

  • Forecasting & Scenario Modeling

    Volume forecasting, drivers analysis, and “what-if” planning to support capacity, investment, and multi-quarter strategy decisions.

  • Decision Science & Business Strategy

    Opportunity sizing, KPI design, and synthesis of complex analyses into clear recommendations that drive action.

  • Experimentation & Causal Measurement

    A/B testing, power analysis, incrementality, and causal frameworks to quantify impact and reduce decision risk.

  • Analytics Products & Platforms

    End-to-end analytics systems: ingestion, modeling layers, quality checks, governance, and self-serve experiences that scale adoption.

  • Data Architecture & Governance

    Designing privacy-safe, access-controlled data foundations (Snowflake/Databricks) that enable trustworthy analytics at scale.

  • Executive Communication

    Structured narratives, crisp visuals, and decision memos that make statistical findings understandable and actionable for senior stakeholders.
